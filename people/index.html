Cash Lab • Founding Director

Dr. Sydney Cash, MD, PhD

Division Chief of MGB Neurology Division of Epilepsy & Neurophysiology • Professor of Neurology, HMS

Division Chief of MGB Neurology Division of Epilepsy and Neurophysiology and Professor of Neurology at Harvard Medical School. He is the founding director of the NeuroHINTS Lab (formerly the NDM Group) and co-director of the Center for Neurotechnology and Neurorecovery (CNTR). His work focuses on understanding human brain physiology at multiple scales, from single neurons to large-scale networks, particularly in the contexts of epilepsy, sleep, and cognition.

Paulk Lab • Principal Investigator

Dr. Angelique Paulk, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Neurology, Massachusetts General Hospital & Harvard Medical School

Assistant Professor and a leading neuroscientist focused on systems neuroscience and the development of next-generation high-density recording technologies. Her lab pioneers the use of Neuropixels and advanced microelectrodes in the human brain. Notably, her NIH-funded research is paving the way for the emerging field of Cancer Neuroscience, exploring how tumor infiltration alters neural activity signatures.

Salami Lab • Principal Investigator

Dr. Pariya Salami, Ph.D.

Instructor in Neurology, Massachusetts General Hospital & Harvard Medical School

Instructor and an investigator focusing on neurophysiology, electroencephalography (EEG), and advanced signal processing. Her lab specializes in deploying sophisticated computational algorithms to extract meaningful patterns from brain activity, significantly contributing to our understanding of epileptic networks and the mechanisms of neuromodulation.

Zelmann Lab • Principal Investigator

Dr. Rina Zelmann, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Neurology, Massachusetts General Hospital & Harvard Medical School

Assistant Professor whose lab operates at the intersection of biomedical engineering and clinical neuroscience. Her research heavily utilizes intracranial EEG to map brain dynamics. She plays a crucial role in analyzing high-frequency oscillations and studying both pathological states like epilepsy and physiological states such as sleep and memory consolidation.

Hadar Lab • Principal Investigator

Dr. Peter Hadar, MD, MS

Instructor in Neurology, Massachusetts General Hospital & Harvard Medical School • Epilepsy Therapeutics & AI

Instructor in Neurology whose lab focuses on Epilepsy Therapeutics & Artificial Intelligence in neurology. His group investigates novel therapeutic strategies for drug-resistant epilepsy alongside clinical applications of deep learning and Large Language Models (LLMs)—including AI-derived neurophysiological biomarkers and frameworks to safely integrate generative AI into epilepsy care, neurological practice, and medical education.
