Cash Lab

Dr. Sydney Cash, MD, PhD

Division Chief of MGB Neurology Division of Epilepsy and Neurophysiology and Professor. He is the founding director of the NDM Group and co-director of the Center for Neurotechnology and Neurorecovery (CNTR). His work focuses on understanding brain physiology at multiple scales, from single neurons to large-scale networks, particularly in the contexts of epilepsy, sleep, and cognition.

Associated Teams:

Epilepsy & Neuromodulation Cognition & Sleep Neurotechnology

Paulk Lab

Dr. Angelique Paulk, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor and a leading neuroscientist focused on systems neuroscience and the development of next-generation high-density recording technologies. Her lab pioneers the use of Neuropixels and advanced microelectrodes in the human brain. Notably, her NIH-funded research is paving the way for the emerging field of Cancer Neuroscience, exploring how tumor infiltration alters neural activity signatures.

Associated Teams:

Cancer Neuroscience Neurotechnology

Salami Lab

Dr. Pariya Salami, Ph.D.

Instructor and an investigator focusing on neurophysiology, electroencephalography (EEG), and advanced signal processing. Her lab specializes in deploying sophisticated computational algorithms to extract meaningful patterns from brain activity, significantly contributing to our understanding of epileptic networks and the mechanisms of neuromodulation.

Associated Teams:

Epilepsy & Neuromodulation

Zelmann Lab

Dr. Rina Zelmann, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor whose lab operates at the intersection of biomedical engineering and clinical neuroscience. Her research heavily utilizes intracranial EEG to map brain dynamics. She plays a crucial role in analyzing high-frequency oscillations and studying both pathological states like epilepsy and physiological states such as sleep and memory consolidation.

Associated Teams:

Cognition & Sleep Epilepsy & Neuromodulation

Hadar Lab

Dr. Peter Hadar, MD, MS

Instructor who leads initiatives at the bleeding edge of Artificial Intelligence in medicine. His lab investigates the clinical applications of deep learning and Large Language Models (LLMs) in neurology. This includes developing AI-derived biomarkers from massive sleep EEG cohorts and engineering frameworks to safely integrate generative AI into neurological practice and medical education.

Associated Teams:

AI in Neurology & Neuroscience
