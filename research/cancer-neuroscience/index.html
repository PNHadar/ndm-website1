Cancer Neuroscience Team

Tumor & Neural Signatures

Neural Activity Signatures of Tumor Infiltration

The intersection of oncology and neurophysiology represents a rapidly expanding frontier. Brain tumors do not exist in isolation; they integrate into functional neural networks. Recent evidence suggests that neuronal activity can directly promote the growth and infiltration of gliomas and other malignant brain tumors.

Led by Dr. Angelique Paulk, our team investigates how tumor infiltration physically alters the surrounding neural tissue and distorts electrical signaling. By deploying high-density Neuropixels probes and advanced microelectrodes in human patients, we map the electrophysiological signatures—the "electrical footprints"—of tumor borders. This high-resolution mapping helps define functional boundaries, providing critical insights that could revolutionize surgical resection and lead to novel, electroceutical therapies targeting the tumor microenvironment.
