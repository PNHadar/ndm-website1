Research & Teams

Our group is organized into five specialized, collaborative teams. Together, we integrate neurophysiology, engineering, clinical neurology, and AI to unravel the complexities of brain function.

Epilepsy & Neuromodulation Team

Investigating the spatial and temporal dynamics of seizure initiation, the fundamentals of cortical oscillations, and how direct electrical stimulation can map and modulate the human brain.

Explore Team

Cognition & Sleep Team

Understanding how memory is consolidated and how information is processed during both waking cognition and the fundamental states of sleep and dreams.

Explore Team

Neurotechnology Team

Developing highly detailed microelectrode recordings and closed-loop brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) to decode pathological states and deliver real-time therapeutic interventions.

Explore Team

Cancer Neuroscience Team

Investigating neural activity signatures of tumor infiltration in the human brain. This emerging field explores the complex interactions between cancer progression and the nervous system.

Explore Team

AI in Neurology & Neuroscience Team

Leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs), prompt engineering, and deep learning techniques to identify biomarkers (e.g., from Sleep EEG) for cognition, disease prediction, and mortality.
