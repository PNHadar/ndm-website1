AI in Neurology & Neuroscience Team

Machine Learning & LLMs

Deep Learning Biomarkers from Clinical EEG

Electroencephalography (EEG) data collected during sleep contains vast amounts of unstructured information regarding brain health. We develop end-to-end deep learning architectures capable of extracting predictive biomarkers from massive multi-cohort EEG datasets. These AI-derived Brain Age indices and biomarker profiles are correlated with cognitive decline, disease progression, and overall mortality risk, proving that scalable machine learning can unlock immense diagnostic value from routine clinical recordings.

Large Language Models (LLMs) & Clinical Workflows

The integration of generative AI into clinical medicine promises to radically improve efficiency and education. Led by researchers like Dr. Peter Hadar, we critically evaluate the performance of advanced LLMs across neurological reasoning tasks. We investigate optimal "prompt engineering" frameworks specifically tailored for neurologists and medical trainees, ensuring that AI tools provide safe, scientifically accurate, and context-aware clinical assistance.

Furthermore, our team explores the nuances of human-AI collaboration in neurology, designing systems that enhance rather than replace physician judgement in complex diagnostic landscapes.
