Decoding & Modulating the Human Brain Across Scales

The NeuroHINTS Lab (Human Intracranial Neurophysiology, Neurotechnology, and Stimulation; formerly the NeuroDynamics & NeuroModulation Group) is a collaborative, team-based research enterprise focused on bringing together experts in neurology, neurosurgery, engineering, and computational neuroscience to understand human brain function under normal and pathological conditions.

We focus on approaches that extract neural information at multiple spatial and temporal scales—from single cortical neurons recorded with high-density Neuropixels and laminar microarrays to large-scale sEEG/ECoG networks, closed-loop electrical stimulation, and clinical AI biomarkers—and combine them into a unified understanding of human brain physiology to design therapeutic interventions and brain-computer neuroprosthetics.

Team 01 • Epilepsy & Stimulation

Epilepsy & Neuromodulation Team

Investigating the spatial and temporal dynamics of seizure initiation and termination, cortical oscillations (alpha rhythms, slow waves, ripples), and direct electrical cortical stimulation (SPES, CCEP, and 100 Hz theta-burst) to map and treat drug-resistant epilepsy.

Oscillations (Alpha / Slow Waves / Ripples) Seizure Initiation & Termination Direct Cortical Stimulation
Team 02 • Sleep & Memory

Cognition & Sleep Team

Understanding how memory is consolidated and how information is processed across waking cognition, NREM sleep, and dreams via travelling cortical spindles, hippocampal ripples, motor replay, and closed-loop stimulation.

Spindles, Ripples & Memory Consolidation Neural Replay & Stimulation Dreams & Epilepsy Sleep
Team 03 • Neural Engineering

Neurotechnology Team

Developing high-density microelectrode recordings (intraoperative human Neuropixels, PtNR grids), micromagnetic coils, and closed-loop brain-computer interfaces (CLoSES, BrainGate) to decode and modulate neural circuits in real time.

Micro-Arrays & Human Neuropixels BrainGate BCIs Closed-Loop Stimulation (CLoSES)
Team 04 • Oncology & Circuits

Cancer Neuroscience Team

NIH-funded research exploring how glioma and brain tumor infiltration alters local cellular and network electrophysiology in the human cortex using intraoperative high-density ECoG and single-unit Neuropixels.

Glioma Infiltration Signatures Intraoperative ECoG & Neuropixels Peritumoral Hyperexcitability
Team 05 • Epilepsy Therapeutics & AI

AI in Neurology & Neuroscience Team

Pioneering epilepsy therapeutics and clinical applications of Large Language Models (LLMs), prompt engineering in neurology, and deep-learning biomarkers from sleep and intracranial EEG for brain age, cognition, and mortality.

Epilepsy Therapeutics & AI LLMs in Clinical Neurology Sleep EEG Deep-Learning Biomarkers

Single Units • Neuropixels • Pt Nanorods • BrainGate BCI

High-Density Micro-Arrays & Single-Neuron Recording in Humans

Using intraoperative Neuropixels probes, flexible platinum nanorod (PtNR) micro-grids, laminar microelectrode arrays, and Utah arrays, we resolve hundreds of individual cortical neurons simultaneously across human cortical layers during language, cognition, focal seizures, and brain tumor resection.

Cortical Layers • Travelling Spindles • Sharp-Wave Ripples • Memory Replay

Cortical Oscillations, Sleep Spindles & Memory Consolidation

By recording across superficial and deep cortical layers simultaneously, our teams have shown how slow oscillations, travelling sleep spindles, and cortical/hippocampal ripples coordinate spike-timing plasticity, motor cortex replay, and memory consolidation during human sleep.

Explore Cognition & Sleep Topics →
Direct Electrical Stimulation • CLoSES Closed-Loop BCI • Seizure Termination

Seizure Initiation, Termination & Closed-Loop Cortical Stimulation

Through single-pulse electrical stimulation (SPES), 100 Hz theta-burst protocols, and our open-source CLoSES (Closed-Loop System for Electrical Stimulation) platform, we map cortico-cortical evoked potentials (CCEPs) and deliver real-time, state-dependent stimulation locked to endogenous oscillations or epileptic discharges.

Epilepsy Therapeutics • Deep Learning EEG Biomarkers • Clinical LLMs

Epilepsy Therapeutics, Deep-Learning EEG Biomarkers & Clinical LLMs

Extending from intracranial physiology to large-scale clinical epilepsy cohorts and sleep EEG repositories, we advance novel epilepsy therapeutics, train deep neural networks to quantify Brain Age and neurological risk, and build validated Large Language Model (LLM) frameworks for clinical neurology.

Cash Lab • Founding Director

Dr. Sydney Cash, MD, PhD

Division Chief of MGB Neurology Division of Epilepsy & Neurophysiology • Professor of Neurology, HMS

Division Chief of MGB Neurology Division of Epilepsy and Neurophysiology and Professor of Neurology at Harvard Medical School. He is the founding director of the NeuroHINTS Lab (formerly the NDM Group) and co-director of the Center for Neurotechnology and Neurorecovery (CNTR). His work focuses on understanding brain physiology at multiple scales, from single neurons to large-scale networks, particularly in the contexts of epilepsy, sleep, and cognition.

Paulk Lab • Principal Investigator

Dr. Angelique Paulk, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Neurology, MGH & Harvard Medical School

Assistant Professor and a leading neuroscientist focused on systems neuroscience and the development of next-generation high-density recording technologies. Her lab pioneers the use of Neuropixels and advanced microelectrodes in the human brain. Notably, her NIH-funded research is paving the way for the emerging field of Cancer Neuroscience, exploring how tumor infiltration alters neural activity signatures.

Salami Lab • Principal Investigator

Dr. Pariya Salami, Ph.D.

Instructor in Neurology, MGH & Harvard Medical School

Instructor and an investigator focusing on neurophysiology, electroencephalography (EEG), and advanced signal processing. Her lab specializes in deploying sophisticated computational algorithms to extract meaningful patterns from brain activity, significantly contributing to our understanding of epileptic networks and the mechanisms of neuromodulation.

Zelmann Lab • Principal Investigator

Dr. Rina Zelmann, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Neurology, MGH & Harvard Medical School

Assistant Professor whose lab operates at the intersection of biomedical engineering and clinical neuroscience. Her research heavily utilizes intracranial EEG to map brain dynamics. She plays a crucial role in analyzing high-frequency oscillations and studying both pathological states like epilepsy and physiological states such as sleep and memory consolidation.

Hadar Lab • Principal Investigator

Dr. Peter Hadar, MD, MS

Instructor in Neurology, MGH & Harvard Medical School • Epilepsy Therapeutics & AI

Instructor in Neurology whose lab focuses on Epilepsy Therapeutics & Artificial Intelligence in medicine. His group investigates novel therapeutic strategies for epilepsy alongside clinical applications of deep learning and Large Language Models (LLMs) in neurology—including AI-derived biomarkers from massive sleep and clinical EEG cohorts and frameworks to safely integrate generative AI into neurological practice and medical education.
