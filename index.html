Decoding the
Human Brain

The NeuroDynamics & NeuroModulation (NDM) group is a collaborative, team-based research enterprise focused on bringing together experts to understand brain function under normal and pathological conditions. We aim to develop advanced techniques for diagnosing and treating devastating neuropsychiatric diseases.

We focus on using approaches which extract information at multiple scales and then combine them into a more complete and meaningful understanding of brain physiology; our research allows us to design therapeutic interventions including brain-computer (machine) neuroprosthetics. To reach these goals we are actively developing new methods (neurotechnology & AI) for measuring and modulating neural activity in the brain and using sophisticated signal processing and machine learning techniques to better understand complex neural signals. We are a founding lab of the Clinical Data Animal Center (CDAC) and the Center for Neurotechnology and Neurorecovery (CNTR). We are also part of the consortium of labs involved in the BrainGate Clinical Trial.

Research Teams

An interdisciplinary approach to extracting information at multiple scales.

Epilepsy & Neuromodulation Team

Investigating the mechanisms of seizure generation, cortical oscillations, and direct electrical stimulation of the human brain.

Cognition & Sleep Team

Studying the role of neural oscillations in memory consolidation, learning, and the fundamental states of sleep and dreams.

Neurotechnology Team

Developing closed-loop brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), highly detailed microelectrode recordings, and novel neuromodulation techniques.

Cancer Neuroscience Team

Investigating neural activity signatures of tumor infiltration in the human brain and the interactions between cancer and the nervous system.

AI in Neurology & Neuroscience Team

Leveraging large language models, prompt engineering, and deep learning biomarkers (like sleep EEG) for cognition, disease prediction, and mortality.
