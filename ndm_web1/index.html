Decoding the Human Brain

NeuroHINTS Labs — Human Intracranial Neurophysiology, NeuroTechnology, and Stimulation

The NeuroHINTS Labs (Human Intracranial Neurophysiology, Neurotechnology, and Stimulation) are a collaborative, team-based research enterprise focused on bringing together experts to understand brain function under normal and pathological conditions. We aim to develop advanced techniques for diagnosing and treating devastating neuropsychiatric diseases.

We focus on using approaches which extract information at multiple scales and then combine them into a more complete and meaningful understanding of brain physiology; our research allows us to design therapeutic interventions including brain-computer (machine) neuroprosthetics. To reach these goals we are actively developing new methods (neurotechnology & AI) for measuring and modulating neural activity in the brain and using sophisticated signal processing and machine learning techniques to better understand complex neural signals. We are a founding lab of the Clinical Data Animation Center (CDAC) and the Center for Neurotechnology and Neurorecovery (CNTR). We are also part of the consortium of labs involved in the BrainGate Clinical Trial.

Division Chief of MGB Neurology Division of Epilepsy & Neurophysiology • Professor of Neurology, HMS

Division Chief of MGB Neurology Division of Epilepsy and Neurophysiology and Professor of Neurology at Harvard Medical School. Paulk Lab

Dr. Angelique Paulk, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Neurology, Massachusetts General Hospital & Harvard Medical School

Assistant Professor with a background in systems neuroscience. She investigates how human brain circuits process information and respond to direct electrical stimulation using advanced microelectrodes including Neuropixels and PtNRGrids. She also leads an NIH-funded research effort in Cancer Neuroscience, studying how brain tumors interact with and alter surrounding neural networks.

Salami Lab

Dr. Pariya Salami, Ph.D.

Instructor in Neurology, Massachusetts General Hospital & Harvard Medical School

Instructor whose research focuses on neurophysiology, EEG, and computational signal processing. She uses intracranial recordings in patients with epilepsy to study how seizures start, spread, and terminate, and how surrounding brain states influence seizure dynamics.

Hadar Lab • Epilepsy Therapeutics, AI, NeuroEconomics

Dr. Peter Hadar, MD, MSc

Instructor in Neurology, HMS • MGH • BWH

Instructor in Neurology focusing on Epilepsy Therapeutics, AI, and NeuroEconomics who applies artificial intelligence, machine learning, and decision science to clinical neurophysiology and medical text. His projects include using deep learning on sleep and clinical EEG to improve epilepsy diagnosis, investigating the neural mechanisms of decision-making (NeuroEconomics), and leveraging Large Language Models to extract clinically meaningful insights from electronic health records.
