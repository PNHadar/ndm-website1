Deep Learning Biomarkers from Clinical EEG

Electroencephalography (EEG) collected during routine sleep and clinical monitoring contains vast reservoirs of latent information regarding structural and functional brain health. We architect end-to-end deep learning models capable of extracting predictive biomarkers from multi-institution EEG repositories. These AI-derived Brain Age indices and physiological embeddings correlate strongly with cognitive decline, neurodegenerative progression, and all-cause mortality risk.

Epilepsy Therapeutics • Generative AI • NeuroEconomics

Epilepsy Therapeutics, Clinical LLMs & NeuroEconomics

Led by Dr. Peter Hadar, MD, MSc, our team investigates novel paradigms in Epilepsy Therapeutics, AI, and NeuroEconomics—critically evaluating the reasoning performance and safety of frontier Large Language Models across complex neurological diagnostic tasks and studying the neural mechanisms of human decision-making and value computation. We develop structured prompt-engineering frameworks tailored for neurologists, epileptologists, and medical trainees, ensuring generative AI tools deliver transparent, evidence-grounded clinical assistance.

Furthermore, we study the human-AI interface in clinical practice—designing decision-support systems that augment physician judgment across electronic health records, neurophysiology reporting, and medical education.
