Interdisciplinary Science & Group Identities

Research Groups & Specialized Teams

The NeuroHINTS Labs are organized into specialized research groups and five collaborative teams. Together, we integrate single-neuron and intracranial electrophysiology, neural engineering, clinical neurology, oncology, and artificial intelligence to decode and modulate the human brain.

Epilepsy & Neuromodulation Team

Investigating the spatial and temporal dynamics of seizure initiation and termination, laminar cortical oscillations (alpha rhythms, slow waves, high-frequency ripples), and direct electrical cortical stimulation (SPES, CCEPs, and 100 Hz theta-burst remodeling) to map and treat drug-resistant epilepsy.

Microelectrode Seizure Dynamics CCEP Network Mapping Theta-Burst Stimulation

Pillar 02 • Cash Lab • Zelmann Lab

Cognition & Sleep Team

Uncovering how memories are consolidated across waking cognition, NREM sleep, and dreams. We track travelling cortical sleep spindles, hippocampal sharp-wave ripples, motor cortex replay, and closed-loop spindle-locked intracranial stimulation in humans.

Travelling Sleep Spindles Sharp-Wave Ripples Neural Replay

Neurotechnology Team

Developing high-density flexible microelectrode arrays (porous Pt nanorods, Neuropixels), microfabricated magnetic stimulation solenoids, and real-time closed-loop brain-computer interfaces (CLoSES & BrainGate) to decode neural states and deliver rapid therapeutic intervention.

Pillar 04 • Paulk Lab • Hadar Lab • NIH-Funded Initiative

Cancer Neuroscience

Investigating the electrophysiological footprints of glioma, brain tumor infiltration, and tumor-related epilepsy in the human cortex. Using intraoperative high-density Neuropixels and microelectrode arrays, we map how malignant tumors remodel local neural circuits and excitability at surgical margins.

Intraoperative Neuropixels Glioma Network Remodeling Tumor-Related Epilepsy Electroceutical Oncology

Pillar 05 • Hadar Lab (Epilepsy Therapeutics, AI, NeuroEconomics) • CDAC

AI in Neurology and Neuroscience of AI

Advancing Epilepsy Therapeutics, AI, and NeuroEconomics: leveraging end-to-end deep learning on massive clinical sleep EEG cohorts to extract biomarkers for epilepsy, brain aging, and cognitive decline, investigating mechanistic interpretability, representation engineering, and the neural mechanisms of decision-making, and pioneering clinical evaluation frameworks for Large Language Models (LLMs) in neurology.
