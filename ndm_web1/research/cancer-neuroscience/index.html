Research Team 04 • Tumor-Neural Circuit Coupling

Cancer Neuroscience Team

Decoding the high-resolution electrophysiological footprints of glioma and brain tumor infiltration in the living human cortex.

NeuroHINTS LABS Tumor Group
Paulk Lab (Lead) Cash Lab NIH-Funded Program
Intraoperative Neuropixels • Glioma Microenvironment

Neural Activity Signatures of Tumor Infiltration

The intersection of oncology and human neurophysiology represents one of medicine's fastest-growing frontiers. Malignant brain tumors do not exist in isolation; gliomas synaptically and electrically integrate into functional neural circuits, where neuronal hyperexcitability can directly accelerate tumor proliferation and invasion.

Led by Dr. Angelique Paulk, our NIH-funded Cancer Neuroscience team investigates how tumor infiltration physically alters surrounding human cortical tissue and distorts single-unit and laminar electrical signaling. By deploying high-density Neuropixels probes and microelectrode arrays during awake and anesthetized craniotomies, we map the precise electrophysiological signatures—the "electrical footprints"—of tumor margins to guide functional surgical resection and pioneer electroceutical therapies targeting the tumor microenvironment.
